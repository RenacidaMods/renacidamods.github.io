Classic PC games, reborn for modern hardware

I'm Ed, and RenacidaMods is where I get old PC games running beautifully on today's machines: smooth frame rates at 4K, fixes, and the original feel kept intact. "Renacida" is Spanish for "reborn".

Everything here is free, and works with your own copy of each game. No game files are ever included.

Latest release

RenacidaMods edition of Magic Carpet 2 HD 0.1, released 26 October 2026.

A small add-on (2.7 MB) for Magic Carpet 2 HD: in-between frames up to 120 fps while the game keeps its original 24 steps a second, smoother creatures and spells, sprites at their intended shape, Direct3D 11, and fixes.

How the results are measured

Every number on this site says what it was measured on and when. So far that's one PC: an NVIDIA RTX 3090, AMD Ryzen 9 5950X and a 42" LG C3 at 3840 x 2160, 120 Hz, on Windows. Reports from other PCs are very welcome on GitHub.

Built on the fan projects

RenacidaMods builds on the fan projects that keep these games alive, and most credit belongs to them. Everything here is open source, so they're welcome to adopt anything they like:

  • Magic Carpet 2 HD by thobbsinteractive and contributors, based on remc2.
  • ROLLER by the FatalDecomp team.
